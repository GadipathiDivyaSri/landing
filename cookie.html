Cookie Policy

Applies to: wrindhaos.in & WrindhaOS App Updated: Current Version Contact: wrindhaos@gmail.com
This Cookie Policy explains how wrindhaos.in and, where applicable, the WrindhaOS mobile application use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and mobile identifiers).

1. Scope

This Cookie Policy explains how wrindhaos.in (the "Website") and, where applicable, the WrindhaOS mobile app use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and mobile identifiers).

The WrindhaOS Android app itself does not use browser cookies, but it may use equivalent local-storage mechanisms for session and authentication tokens; references to "cookies" below apply to the Website unless stated otherwise.

2. What Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies on the Website into three specific groups:

Strictly Necessary

Required for the Website to function, such as session integrity, security authentication, and remembering your cookie consent choice. These cannot be disabled.

Preference Cookies

Remember custom settings such as language selection, layout preferences, or display parameters to ensure a consistent browsing experience.

Analytics (Optional)

Help us understand aggregate Website usage, such as which pages are visited, to improve performance. These are only set with your consent where required by law.

3. In-App Local Storage

The mobile app stores your authentication session and certain user preferences locally on your device so you remain signed in and your offline settings persist reliably.

Non-Advertising Guarantee: This local storage mechanism is strictly functional and necessary for the app to operate. It is not a third-party tracking or advertising cookie.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Website from functioning correctly.

Where we deploy a cookie consent banner, you can update your consent preferences at any time via the cookie settings links available across the site.

5. Do Not Track

Our Website does not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals, as no universal industry standard for interpreting and handling them has been established or adopted.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect evolving technical implementations or legal requirements. The current, authoritative version is always accessible at:
