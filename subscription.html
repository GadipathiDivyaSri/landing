Billing & Plan Structure

WrindhaOS Subscription Policy

Billing Gateway: Google Play Billing Contact: wrindhaos@gmail.com

1. Plans & Features

WrindhaOS currently offers two plans tailored for individuals, students, and ambitious professionals:

Free Tier

₹0 / month — Essential daily productivity starter pack:

  • 2 Habits: Active habit streak tracking
  • 2 Subjects: Academic syllabus & unit manager
  • Interactive Calendar: Timetables & daily schedule
  • To-Do List: Daily task management
  • Core Daily Dashboard & offline local storage

Premium (Monthly or Yearly)

₹49 / month — The complete, unlimited personal OS:

  • Everything Unlimited: Unlimited habits, subjects, & tasks
  • Career Roadmap: Milestones & professional skill paths
  • Expense Tracker: Financial awareness & budgets
  • Private Journal: Encrypted reflections & mood logs
  • Priority Matrix: Eisenhower 4-quadrant system
  • Full Study Tools: Pomodoro focus centre & unit logs
  • Deep Analytics: Growth charts & performance metrics

2. How Billing Works

Premium subscriptions are sold and billed exclusively through Google Play Billing, using the payment method on file with your Google Play / Google Payments account.

We receive subscription status, plan, purchase token, and renewal information from Google Play to activate your Premium features — we do not receive or store your card or bank details.

3. Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or yearly, as selected) unless cancelled before the renewal date. You will be charged through your Google Play account at the then-current price unless you cancel.

4. Managing Your Subscription

You can view, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time via Google Play:

  • Play Store app: Menu → Subscriptions
  • Web browser: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions
  • In-app: The in-app subscription screen links directly to Google Play's subscription management interface.

5. What Happens When You Downgrade or Cancel

  • Your Premium access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • After that, your account reverts to the Free plan.
  • If your Free-tier usage exceeds Free-plan limits (e.g., more active habits/subjects than the Free cap allows), existing items are preserved but you may be unable to add new items until you either remove some or resubscribe.
  • Your data (journal, habits, goals, etc.) is not deleted when you downgrade — only Premium feature access changes.

6. Failed Payments

If a renewal payment fails, Google Play will retry according to its own retry schedule. If payment ultimately cannot be collected, your subscription will be marked accordingly and your account will revert to the Free plan at the end of the grace period Google Play provides.

7. Price Changes

We may change subscription pricing from time to time. Existing subscribers will be given advance notice as required by Google Play policy before a price change takes effect on their next renewal, and may cancel before the new price applies.

8. Refunds

Refund requests are handled per our Refund & Cancellation Policy and are ultimately subject to Google Play's refund process.

9. Taxes

Displayed subscription prices are inclusive/exclusive of applicable taxes as shown at checkout in Google Play, which handles tax calculation and remittance for digital subscriptions per its own merchant-of-record arrangements where applicable.
